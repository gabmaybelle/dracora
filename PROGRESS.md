# Progress

What's done, what's broken, what's next. Newest entry first. Each session adds a row before it
ends, saying what changed, what the game tests counted, what the proof run was (the feature off,
which checks fail, code put back), what was learned, and what was deliberately not done.

## Where things stand

- **What it does so far:** a dragon you can summon (`/summon dracora:dragon` or its spawn egg). It
  walks, looks around, and sits or follows when tamed; its owner's empty hand sits it down and
  stands it up. Its colour comes from the biome it appears in (a first draft of each palette; End
  colours are separate and never appear in the Overworld). An egg block that carries a colour:
  set down beside a lit campfire or lava it gathers warmth, cracks twice and hatches after a
  Minecraft day of warmth into a baby dragon of its colour, owned by whoever set the egg down
  (there or away). A plain egg takes its colour from the biome it is set down in; a broken egg
  drops with its colour. Three stages, a Minecraft day each for the first two, by time alone: a
  small hatchling that walks, sits and follows; a juvenile its owner can ride and hop with (a leap
  up and forward, then a glide down); an adult that flies with its owner at up to 15 blocks a
  second, steered by where they look. Only the owner rides or steers: their empty hand mounts, and
  with sneak held (or for a hatchling) sits it down or stands it up. No dragon falls faster than
  a glide or is hurt by a fall. A dragon looks like a small Ender Dragon: it uses the game's own
  Ender Dragon model at half size and a grey copy of its texture, made in the game and tinted by
  the dragon's colour, with the glowing eyes; it stands, walks, sits and folds its wings, which
  the Ender Dragon never does. Nests generate, rarely, on high peaks and in old-growth forests:
  a stone-rimmed bowl with one guarded egg on hay and a wild adult beside it, both in one of the
  biome's colours. The egg can't be broken or blown out of the nest until its parent trusts the
  player: three calm visits (half a minute near it, one counted per Minecraft day, each player's
  own). Sprinting within 12 blocks or walking within 4 without sneaking scares the parent into
  the air for half a minute and loses the visit under way; hurting it loses an earned visit. When
  the egg is gone the parent flies off for good. End nests do the same on the outer End islands'
  highlands, built of end stone and purpur with the egg on obsidian, with parents and eggs in the
  End's five colours. Every dragon glows faintly (never drawn darker than light 9 of 15).
  Two tamed adults fed raw fish lay an egg in their blended colour (one in 20 a rare pearl or
  obsidian), then rest a Minecraft day.
  Fabric and NeoForge on 26.3. The egg's model and
  textures, the spawn egg and the icon are placeholders until Gabby's art.
- **Broken:** nothing known. Riding and flight have run in a real single-player game and on a
  dedicated server with two players and flying forbidden (no kicks, no movement warnings). What
  the second player sees of someone else's flight has not been looked at yet. The new look has
  only been seen on Fabric: NeoForge has no in-game test here, though its jar builds and loads on
  a server. The poses are a first pass: folded wings stand tall like sails, the walk is a simple
  leg swing, and the neck and tail don't yet trail through turns in flight. A nest has been
  visited by a real player in a single-player game on Fabric, not yet on a server with others
  watching. A parent coming down folds its wings a moment before its feet touch.
  Breeding has run in a real single-player game, not yet on a server.
- **Next:** session 7.
  The compat runs with Terralith and Stellarity still have to join CI.

## 0.1.0 scope (locked, 2026-10-21)

Everything below ships together in 0.1.0 (Gabby's call). New ideas go to "Later", never here.

- Overworld nests: rare, on high peaks and in old-growth forests, one egg each.
- End nests on remote outer islands, with the End palette (violets, blacks, shimmer).
- A shy wild parent near each nest: sprinting, attacking or rushing close scares it off; calm
  visits earn its trust until it lets you take the egg. It never fights.
- Eggs hatch near warmth; the hatchling bonds to the player who hatched it.
- Colour from birthplace (biome palettes); End colours never overlap Overworld ones.
- Three stages: hatchling follows, juvenile can be ridden for short hops, adult flies at about
  elytra gliding speed. Sit and follow. Only the owner rides.
- Breeding: colours blend, with a small chance of a rare mutation (pearl, obsidian...).
- A raised dragon that dies is gone for good.

Later: clues near nests (shed scales, scorch marks, claw-marked trees), sighting times, saddlebags,
abilities, more colours.
