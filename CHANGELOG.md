# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- A dragon, named Dracora in the game, that you can summon with a command or its spawn egg. It
  walks, looks around, and sits or follows when tamed; a summoned dragon is wild and stays so,
  since a dragon is only ever yours when you hatch it. It looks like a small Ender Dragon in its
  own colour: it uses the game's Ender Dragon model and texture, so a resource pack that changes
  the Ender Dragon changes Dracora's dragons too.
- Each dragon's colour comes from the biome it appears in: snowy, jungle, warm, forest, water and
  plains colours in the Overworld, and End colours that never appear anywhere else.
- A dragon egg. Set one down beside a lit campfire or lava and it warms up, cracks twice and
  hatches after about a Minecraft day of warmth. The hatchling wears the egg's colour and belongs to
  whoever set the egg down, even if they are away when it hatches. A plain egg takes its colour
  from the biome it is set down in; a broken egg keeps its colour.
- Dragons grow up: a hatchling for one Minecraft day, a juvenile for the next, then an adult.
- Riding, for the owner only. An empty hand mounts a juvenile or an adult; with sneak held (or for
  a hatchling) it sits the dragon down or stands it up. A juvenile hops with the jump key. An adult
  takes off with it and flies where its rider looks, at up to 15 blocks a second: about an
  elytra's glide, never a rocket's speed.
- Dragons glide down rather than fall, and a fall never hurts them.
- Nests, rare, on high peaks and in old-growth forests: a ring of stone with one egg on a bed of
  hay and a wild parent beside it. The egg can't be taken, by hand or any other way, until the
  parent trusts you, and a blast leaves it standing.
  Nests keep to dry ground, never on a pond or river.
- Earning that trust: stay near the parent, calmly, for half a minute; one visit counts each
  Minecraft day, and the third earns the egg. Sprinting nearby, or walking right up to it without
  sneaking, scares it into the air for a while and loses the visit you were on; hurting it loses
  a visit you had earned. It never fights back. Trust is each player's own. Once the egg is
  taken, the parent flies away for good.
- End nests, rare, on the outer End islands' highlands: an end stone bowl with a purpur rim and
  the egg on obsidian, watched by an End-born parent. Its egg hatches an End-coloured dragon.
- Two more End colours, a chorus violet and a void blue, beside the violet, near-black and pale
  lilac. No End colour is ever an Overworld one.
- Every dragon glows faintly: it is never drawn darker than about two thirds of full light, so
  dragons show at night and in caves.
- Breeding: two grown dragons of yours, each fed raw salmon or cod, lay an egg in a blend of their
  colours. One egg in 20 is a rare colour instead, pearl or obsidian. A pair then rests for a
  Minecraft day. Fish never hurries a young dragon's growth.
- A guide book, A Keeper's Notes, on a lectern in every nest: how to earn the egg, hatch it, raise
  and fly your dragon, and breed dragons.
- Dragons walk with full strides, lifting their feet as they step.
- Art: the mod's icon, a rounded egg with a shell plated like a dragon's hide (in its colour), and
  the spawn egg.
- Settings in config/dracora.properties: nestSpacing (how far apart nests are; 0 turns them off),
  flightSpeed (blocks a second, 15 by default) and juvenileRiding (true by default).
- Tested with two players on a Fabric server (riding, flying, breeding and nests, with no kicks for
  flying), and with Terralith and Stellarity.
